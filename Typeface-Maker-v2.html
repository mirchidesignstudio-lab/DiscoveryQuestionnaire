How it works

  1. Each character can be marked only once — hover a green one and press × to free it.
  2. Capital and small letters are separate slots; switch with the tabs above.
  3. Shapes are auto-traced to vectors and fitted to the base font's metrics.
  4. Fine-tune tracing, snap-to-font, weight and points in Glyphs.
  5. Wheel = zoom · Space / right-drag = pan · Esc = clear.
